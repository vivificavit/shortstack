# shortstack privacy policy

_Last updated: November 7, 2026_

shortstack is an app for parents to keep track of their kids' allowance, spending and savings goals. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into it.

## The short version

shortstack doesn't collect, send or share any of your information. Everything you enter stays on your phone. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no internet access.

## What the app stores

You enter all of the information yourself, and it is stored only on your device:

- the names you give each kid, which can be first names or nicknames
- income and expense entries: amounts, dates, descriptions and categories
- savings goals, repeating entries and the categories and icons you choose
- simple app settings, such as which kid was selected last

shortstack doesn't ask for any device permissions. It can't see your contacts, location, photos, camera, microphone or files, and it doesn't use advertising IDs or other device identifiers.

## What leaves your phone

**Nothing is sent to us.** shortstack has no servers, and the app can't connect to the internet.

Your data leaves the device only in these cases, and you control each one:

- **Android backup.** If backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include shortstack's data in your device backup, which Google stores in your Google account, and in transfers to a new phone. Google handles this under its own privacy policy, and we can't see this data. You can turn backup off in your phone's settings.
- **Files you export.** The app's "Export backup" and "Export spreadsheet (CSV)" options save a file to a location you choose. After that, the file is yours to keep, share or delete. It contains the data listed above.

## Children

shortstack is meant to be used by parents and other adults. A parent may enter a child's name and money records, but this information stays on the parent's device and is never sent to us or anyone else. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children, or from anyone else.

## Deleting your data

- To remove one kid and their records, use **Edit kids** in the app.
- To remove everything, uninstall the app or clear its storage in your phone's settings. Backups you exported and Android device backups are kept separately, and you can delete those yourself.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new "Last updated" date. If the app ever starts collecting or sharing data, the policy will be updated before that version is released.
